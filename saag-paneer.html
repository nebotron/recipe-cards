Saag Paneer
1 tsp. turmeric mix toss and marinate brown over medium-high heat;
remove from pan		 add paneer
1/2 tsp. cayenne
1 tsp. salt
3 Tbs. vegetable oil
12 oz. paneer (or tofu), cubed
1-1/2 Tbs. vegetable oil sauté in same pan until soft and golden (add a few Tbs. water if drying out) cook and stir 3–5 min. mix stir; heat through 10–15 min. mix
1 white onion, diced
1 Tbs. ginger, diced
4 cloves garlic, diced
1/2 tsp. garam masala
2 tsp. coriander
1 tsp. cumin
1 lb. frozen spinach
salt, to taste
1/2 cup water
1/2 cup yogurt

The original 4.5 Tbs. of vegetable oil is split: 3 Tbs. for the paneer marinade and the remaining 1-1/2 Tbs. for sautéing the aromatics.
